If you are searching for EA SPORTS UFC 5 system requirements to determine whether your gaming PC can handle the Octagon, the most important fact is that there is no official PC version of the game. EA SPORTS UFC 5 launched exclusively on ninth-generation consoles, meaning the only way to play is on a PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X/S. This matters because you do not need to worry about CPU, GPU, or RAM benchmarks—your hardware requirements are limited to owning a compatible console, plus an internet connection for online features.

## Is EA SPORTS UFC 5 Available on PC?

According to official EA support documentation and the game’s Wikipedia entry, EA SPORTS UFC 5 was released on October 27, 2023, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. The PlayStation Store product page also confirms the title is "Available on PS5" and notes it is "PS5 Pro Enhanced." 

While some third-party key resellers list a PC version marked as "out of stock" with a placeholder release date, these storefronts are not official sources. First-party materials from Electronic Arts do not include a PC port, so players should not expect to download or install UFC 5 on a Windows computer. For the definitive list of supported hardware, you can review the [EA Help getting started guide](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/ufc/ufc-5/intro-to-ufc-5/), which states that UFC 5 is available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## Verified Platform Requirements for UFC 5

Because UFC 5 is a console exclusive, its "system requirements" are defined by the consoles it supports rather than by internal PC components. To play the game, you must have one of the following:

* **PlayStation 5:** The standard PS5 or the PS5 Pro. The PlayStation Store labels the PS5 version “PS5 Pro Enhanced,” although the provided listing does not detail the specific enhancements.
* **Xbox Series X or Xbox Series S:** EA lists the Xbox Series X|S as a supported platform.

In terms of local play, the PlayStation Store lists support for 1–2 players, so you can compete against a friend on the same console. There is no requirement for high-end graphics cards, processors, or custom cooling solutions.

## Online Play and Connectivity Requirements

If you plan to step into Online Career Mode or participate in live Fight Week challenges, you will need an active internet connection. The PlayStation Store specifically notes that "PS Plus [is] required for online play" on PS5, so a PlayStation Plus subscription is mandatory for multiplayer matchmaking on Sony’s console. The provided first-party sources do not list a separate Xbox subscription term, so check the current Xbox store and platform requirements before subscribing to anything.

UFC 5 also receives regular content updates—such as the Patch 1.27 notes and new roster additions like Reinier de Ridder—so an internet connection is necessary to download patches and keep your fighter lineup current.

## Storage and Update Considerations

Official EA and PlayStation materials provided do not list a specific install size or storage requirement for UFC 5, so we cannot state an exact gigabyte figure. Because the game uses the Frostbite engine to deliver high-fidelity character likenesses and Arenas, you should check your console’s available storage before installing the base game and its updates.

As always, check the PlayStation Store or Xbox Store product page for the most current download size before you buy. Keeping your system software up to date will also ensure the best possible performance when running the game.

No. UFC 5 is a ninth-generation exclusive. Official platform listings only include the PS5 and Xbox Series X/S, so older consoles are not supported.
