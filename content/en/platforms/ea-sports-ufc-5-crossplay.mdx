## The Short Answer on EA SPORTS UFC 5 Crossplay

If you and your friends own different consoles, you need to know one thing before buying: EA SPORTS UFC 5 crossplay is not supported. PS5 and Xbox Series X|S players are restricted to their own console families and cannot fight each other online. That means you must own the same platform as your rivals to step into the Octagon together.

UFC 5 is a next-generation-only release. EA’s own [UFC 5 getting started guide](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/ufc/ufc-5/intro-to-ufc-5/) lists the supported platforms as PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The explicit statement that the two console families cannot play together comes from the EssentiallySports report cited below, not from that EA platform list. If you were hoping to settle a rivalry across consoles, you’ll need to meet on the same system instead.

## Why EA SPORTS UFC 5 Does Not Support Crossplay

The lack of cross-platform play is tied to how the game was built. According to a [report from EssentiallySports](https://www.essentiallysports.com/ufc-mma-news-is-ufc-five-cross-play/), UFC 5 does not support cross-platform gameplay, meaning PS5 players cannot compete against Xbox Series X/S players, and vice versa. The outlet notes that EA’s next-generation-only focus was cited as a reason for prioritizing graphical fidelity, frame rate, and performance over cross-platform play.

That performance-first approach matches EA’s description of the game. According to EA Help, UFC 5 is powered by Frostbite and delivers graphics upgrades, all-new damage systems, and doctor stoppages that affect gameplay. These features aim to make the experience as authentic as possible, which helps explain why the developers concentrated on a single console family’s hardware rather than bridging two ecosystems.

## Available Platforms and Crossplay Status

The verified platform list is short and specific. UFC 5 is available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no evidence of support for older consoles or additional platforms in EA’s official getting-started materials.

| Platform | Availability | Online Competition |
|---|---|---|
| PlayStation 5 | Available | Restricted to PS5 players |
| Xbox Series X\|S | Available | Restricted to Xbox players |

Because crossplay is unavailable, keep these points in mind:
- You can only match with players on your own console family.
- Online Career Mode lets you challenge players all over the world, but only within your platform’s ecosystem.
- If your friends are on a different console, you will need to play on separate systems or use the same console to compete.

## Online Play, Modes, and Progression in UFC 5

Once you are on the correct platform, online and offline options both reward progress. EA Help highlights Online Career Mode, where you can challenge players globally, as well as Fight Week, which syncs challenges with real-world UFC Pay-Per-View events. Career Mode puts you under Coach Davis’ guidance as you build a custom fighter.

Progression works across modes. EA’s beginner tips explain that you earn XP by playing any mode, with different amounts given for online and offline matches. Completing challenge board goals is a fast way to earn a solid amount of XP at once. XP feeds Punch Card level progression, unlocking rewards such as UFC Coins, which can be spent in the Store on gear, accessories, emotes, and sometimes Alter Egos.

When creating a fighter, you choose one of five styles: Kickboxer, Boxer, MMA, Wrestler, or Jiu-jitsu. Each has unique strengths and weaknesses, so review your fighter’s perks and ratings before competing. Training Manual and Practice Mode in the Learn tab are the best places to master controls without tiring your fighter.
