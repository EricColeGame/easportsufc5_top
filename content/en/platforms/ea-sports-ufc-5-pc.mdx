## What the PS5 and Xbox Series X/S Editions Include

Because there is no PC build to buy, the practical UFC 5 experience is the console edition. The game uses the Real Impact System to make damage matter, and it is rated M for Mature 17+ for blood, strong language, and violence.

- **Authentic damage:** Cuts, bruising, and swelling accumulate from strikes given and taken. The PlayStation Store and GameStop material cite over 64,000 possible combinations of facial damage, and severe injuries can trigger doctor checks or stoppages.
- **Seamless Submissions:** Grappling was revamped with faster animations and no submission mini-games, creating a smoother ground game.
- **Presentation:** Frostbite powers the game for the first time in franchise history, and Cinematic K.O. Replays highlight fight-ending finishes.
- **Modes:** Career Mode returns with Coach Davis and a G.O.A.T. goal. Online Career Mode adds skill-based matchmaking, division progression, and title chases across four divisions. Fight Week ties challenges to real-world UFC events.
- **Editions on PS5:** The PlayStation Store lists Standard at $69.99, Deluxe at $79.99, and Ultimate at $99.99, with higher editions adding fighters and Alter Ego bundles.
