## EA SPORTS UFC 5 Controls PS5: The Complete PS5 Button Layout

If you are looking for EA SPORTS UFC 5 controls PS5, stepping into the Octagon can feel overwhelming until you know which button does what on your DualSense. The difference between landing a fight-ending strike and getting caught in a submission often comes down to learning the control scheme before the bell rings. On PS5, EA SPORTS UFC 5 maps movement to the left stick, head movement to the right stick, and uses the shoulder buttons as modifiers for blocks, grapples, and strikes, with the face buttons handling punches, kicks, knees, and elbows.

EA SPORTS UFC 5 launched on October 27, 2023, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. According to its [EA Sports UFC 5 Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EA_Sports_UFC_5), it was the first series entry built on the Frostbite engine, bringing a revamped damage system and seamless submissions. EA’s official controls hub and the PlayStation Store page both returned access errors during research, so the verified DualSense mappings below come from third-party control guides by [Ginx.tv’s UFC 5 controls guide](https://www.ginx.tv/en/controls-2) and [RealSport101’s UFC 5 controls guide](https://realsport101.com/article/ufc-5-controls-guide).

## Choosing Your Control Scheme and Game Style in UFC 5

Before memorizing inputs, pick a game style and grapple control type. Ginx.tv notes that UFC 5 offers four fight styles:

- **Competitive:** The balanced default used for online competitive modes.
- **Simulation:** Real damage, real stamina, and real-time consequences.
- **Knockout:** Emphasizes knockout power by reducing health recovery.
- **Stand and Bang:** Striking only, with grappling, takedowns, clinch, and submissions disabled.

If you are frustrated by grappling, Knockout or Stand and Bang let you lean on strikes. For grappling itself, choose **Legacy Grapple Controls** for maximum depth, **Grapple Assist** for a simplified ground game, or **Hybrid** to blend them. Control settings can be changed at any time, but Career Mode locks your chosen game style when you create a save.

- **Body Kicks:** Tap L2 + X or L2 + Circle

- **Minor Lunge:** Flick Left Stick
- **Major Lunge:** Hold L1 + Move Left Stick
- **Pivot Lunge:** Hold L1 + Move Right Stick
- **Signature Evade:** Hold L1 + Flick Left Stick

## Frequently Asked Questions About EA SPORTS UFC 5 Controls on PS5

### What are the basic EA SPORTS UFC 5 controls PS5 players should learn first?

Start with movement on the left stick and head movement on the right stick. Then learn the face buttons: Square (left punch), Triangle (right punch), X (left leg kick), and Circle (right leg kick). After that, practice holding L1, L2, R1, and R2 to modify strikes, blocks, and grapples.

### Does UFC 5 have a simplified control option on PS5?

Yes. Grapple Assist gives simplified ground inputs using the left stick: Up to get up, Left for submission, and Right for ground and pound. Hybrid blends those inputs with Legacy controls. You can change control settings anytime, but Career Mode game styles are locked when the save is created.

### Where can I check a fighter’s specific moves in EA SPORTS UFC 5?

Pause the match and open the moves list to confirm which strikes, takedowns, and submissions your fighter has. Third-party guides from Ginx.tv and RealSport101 also recommend practicing in Career Mode tutorials or offline bouts to build muscle memory.

### When was EA SPORTS UFC 5 released, and does it use a new engine?

It released on October 27, 2023, and was the first series entry developed with Frostbite instead of Ignite, according to its Wikipedia entry. This engine change supported the new damage system and seamless submissions referenced in the control guides.
