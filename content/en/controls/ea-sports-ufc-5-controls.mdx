Stepping into the Octagon in EA SPORTS UFC 5 is punishing if you mash buttons and hope for a highlight-reel knockout. The game splits every fight into stand-up, clinch, and ground phases, and each phase relies on its own strikes, modifiers, and grapple inputs on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Below is a verified EA SPORTS UFC 5 controls button map, plus the official assist and accessibility options that simplify the learning curve.

The button inputs below come from [GINX TV’s October 2023 UFC 5 controls guide](https://www.ginx.tv/en/controls-2) and [RealSport101’s UFC 5 controls guide](https://realsport101.com/article/ufc-5-controls-guide), updated June 2026. ## Choose Your Control Scheme and Game Style

EA SPORTS UFC 5 controls change depending on the control scheme and game style you pick before the fight. According to GINX TV’s guide, you can choose from these game styles:

- **Competitive:** Balanced option used by default for all online competitive modes
- **Simulation:** Real damage, real stamina, in real-time
- **Knockout:** Emphasizes knockout power by reducing health recovery
- **Stand and Bang:** Striking only, with all grappling, takedowns, clinch, and submissions disabled

Those game styles are locked to each Career Mode save, but you can change your controller settings at any time. For grappling, you must decide between **Legacy Grapple Controls**, the simplified **Grapple Assist**, or **Hybrid**, which blends both. GINX notes that mastering Legacy gives an online edge, while Grapple Assist or Hybrid is the best option for most players.

EA’s official accessibility resources add that the game also includes **Simple Controls** and two assists: **Grapple** and **Defensive Grapple**. Simple Controls let the AI choose the best available strikes, combinations, and actions, but this setting is not available in Online Modes. When either assist is on, the left stick is used for grappling moves and the AI determines direction; EA warns this can be challenging online against opponents using the Hybrid or Legacy Grapple HUD.

RealSport101’s guide also notes that a fighter’s style matters. For example, a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu fighter is very effective in grappling but can lose to a more balanced kickboxer on the feet, so try several fighters to find one that matches your approach.

## Accessibility and Assist Features for UFC 5 Controls

EA’s official accessibility resources confirm several features that affect controls and readability. You can select a game difficulty to change how challenging AI opponents are. You can also enable Simple Controls for AI-selected strikes and combinations, or toggle the Grapple and Defensive Grapple assists for simplified grappling.

Other verified accessibility options include:

- **Colorblind Filters:** Choose Normal, Protanopia, Deuteranopia, or Tritanopia
- **Lock Camera Side Positioning:** Keeps the Red Corner fighter on the left and Blue Corner on the right, though it may cause unnatural camera movement
- **Contrast and Brightness:** Adjust sliders to change in-game visual settings
- **Subtitles:** Enable subtitles for cinematic sequences

## EA SPORTS UFC 5 Controls FAQ

### Where can I view the official EA SPORTS UFC 5 controls and assist details?

EA’s official UFC 5 accessibility resources outline Simple Controls, Grapple and Defensive Grapple assists, and difficulty options. Full button-by-button EA SPORTS UFC 5 controls maps for PS5 and Xbox are documented by GINX TV and RealSport101.

### Are PS5 and Xbox inputs for EA SPORTS UFC 5 controls identical?

No, the actions are the same, but the buttons differ. PS5 uses Square, Triangle, Circle, X, and L1/L2/R1/R2, while Xbox uses X, Y, B, A, and LB/LT/RB/RT. Stick inputs and modifier concepts are shared across both consoles.

### What is the easiest way to learn EA SPORTS UFC 5 controls?

Start in Career Mode, which includes early tutorials and weekly Sparring drills that teach different aspects of fighting. Turn on Grapple Assist or Hybrid for simplified grappling, then practice the stand-up, clinch, and ground tables above.

### Can Simple Controls be used in online UFC 5 matches?

No. EA’s official resources state Simple Controls are not available in Online Modes; they are intended for local or offline competitive play where the AI helps with strikes and combinations.
