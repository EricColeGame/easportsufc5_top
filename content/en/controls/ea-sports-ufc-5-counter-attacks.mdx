## How Head Movement Creates EA SPORTS UFC 5 Counter Attacks

In UFC 5, Head Movement is the official defensive tool that keeps you in range while avoiding damage. According to [EA’s official UFC 5 Striking Defense head movement tutorial](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ufc/ufc-5/tips-and-tricks-hub/how-to-use-head-movement), you use the right stick to move your head in different directions. Your fighter can quickly avoid a strike without moving out of striking range, which is what sets up counter attacks.

### Official Head-Movement Counter Combinations

EA confirms these two counter examples in the tutorial:

- **Move your head to the right:** use a **Left Hook** to counter quickly.
- **Move your head back:** use **Straights** to counter quickly.
- **Find more:** Practice Mode can be used to discover all possible head-movement strike combinations.

These are the only counter-attack inputs officially verified in the accessible EA material.

## Community Counter-Punching Strategies for UFC 5

Beyond the official tutorial, a [community counter-punching guide for UFC 5 from ComboLab](https://combolab.pro/blog/counter-punching-ufc5) (published May 1, 2026) describes broader counter-punching concepts. These are community-sourced techniques, not first-party EA tutorials, so treat them as player-tested advice rather than confirmed game documentation. The guide explains that counter-punching works because an opponent’s strike commits weight forward, briefly opens the guard, and cannot be canceled mid-animation.

### Key Counter Combos From the Guide

- **Cross counter:** When the opponent throws a jab, their extended lead arm briefly exposes the right side of the chin. Fire a cross into that opening before the jab retracts, then extend into **Cross → Lead Hook → Body Shot**.
- **Check hook:** As the opponent surges forward with a jab or cross, pivot slightly outward and throw a hook, letting their forward momentum carry them into the punch.
- **Body kick counter:** Opponents who throw wide hooks expose the midsection on the follow-through, so time a body kick as the hook extends.
- **Leg kick counter:** A leg kick thrown as the opponent steps in can land on the planted leg because they cannot check while attacking.
- **Head kick counter:** After a committed cross, slip outside and fire a head kick as they recover. The guide calls this high risk but high damage.

## Building a Counter-Punching Game Plan in UFC 5

The same community guide stresses that counter-punching is a plan, not just a reaction. Its game-plan tips include:

- **Control distance:** stay at the edge of the opponent’s range—close enough to counter, far enough that the attack does not fully land.
- **Be patient:** you are baiting, not waiting; avoid attacking first most of the time.
- **Read patterns:** most players repeat combos, so after one round you can set the counter before they throw.
- **Pick moments:** do not try to counter every attack; some strikes are too fast or leave you badly positioned.
- **Use recovery data:** the guide suggests checking your fighter’s fastest-recovery strikes in its own combo database, because those are the best counter-combo starters. This refers to the third-party site’s database, not an in-game EA feature.
