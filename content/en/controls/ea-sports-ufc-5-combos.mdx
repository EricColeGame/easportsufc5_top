## Official EA Strike Roles for Building Combinations

The official EA SPORTS UFC 5 “Punches and Kicks” tutorial explains how core strikes function, which is the foundation of any combination. Rather than publishing a list of button-input combo strings in the retrieved material, EA describes the tactical role of each strike. Use the table below to compare verified roles and usage notes.

| Strike | Range & Purpose | Usage Notes |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| Straight | Mostly an intercepting strike | Used as the start of a combination; can intercept Hooks or Overhands |
| Hook | Close-range, heavy damage | Effective against opponents moving left or right; Body Hooks can be used in succession within combos for high damage |
| Uppercut | Close-range counter | Counters an opponent leaning forward or attempting a takedown; Body Uppercuts are powerful even as a single strike |
| Roundhouse Kick | Heavy-damage strike | Can end fights if landed cleanly, but costs a lot of stamina, decreases Maximum Stamina if it misses, and has high strike vulnerability |

Because Straights naturally open combinations and Body Hooks can follow one another, a simple, evidence-based approach is to intercept or initiate with a Straight and then look for close-range Body Hook opportunities. Uppercuts are best kept as reactive tools when your opponent leans in or shoots for a takedown, while Roundhouse Kicks are high-risk, high-reward strikes that demand good distance management to avoid being countered.
