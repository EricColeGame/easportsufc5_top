Mastering the ground game is essential for victory in the Octagon, and understanding the EA SPORTS UFC 5 submission system can mean the difference between a tapout and a wasted opportunity. This matters because successfully applying and finishing submissions requires precise timing and an awareness of your opponent's defensive resources. Based on official guidance, finishing a submission attempt requires transitioning to the Submission Finish, but it will only succeed if your opponent has no Submission Resilience left and misses their denial.

## How to Execute the Submission Finish

According to the [official EA SPORTS UFC 5 Submission Offense tutorial](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ufc/ufc-5/tips-and-tricks-hub/submissions-hub/submission-offense-hub), you need to transition to the **Submission Finish** to complete the attempt. However, simply initiating the move is not enough. The submission will only finalize if two specific conditions are met:

- Your opponent has **no Submission Resilience remaining**.
- Your opponent **misses the denial** input.

If both of these criteria are satisfied, your fighter will successfully execute the submission and win the fight.

## Draining Stamina with Submission Struggle

Grappling exchanges are as much about stamina management as they are about positioning. The **Submission Struggle** mechanic allows you to press your advantage on the ground. By holding the struggle input, you will drain the stamina of both fighters simultaneously. 

This tactic is especially useful for wearing down a defensive opponent. A critical rule to remember is that **fighters cannot deny at zero stamina**. Therefore, depleting the stamina pool can leave your opponent completely unable to defend themselves, setting you up for a fight-ending finish.

## Countering Fakes and Cancels

While the Submission Struggle is a powerful tool for draining stamina, it is not without risk. If your opponent anticipates your struggle, they can neutralize your efforts. The official tutorial notes that this tactic can be countered by **faking and cancelling moves**. Keeping your grappling unpredictable will help you avoid being baited into a counter.

Here is a quick reference for the core submission actions and their verified effects:

| Submission Action | Mechanic / Requirement | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Submission Finish** | Opponent has no Submission Resilience and misses denial | Secures a submission victory |
| **Submission Struggle** | Hold the struggle input | Drains both fighters' stamina; no denial at zero stamina |
| **Fake and Cancel** | Defensive maneuver | Counters the Submission Struggle tactic |
