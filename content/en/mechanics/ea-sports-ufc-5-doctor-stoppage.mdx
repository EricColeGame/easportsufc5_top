In EA SPORTS UFC 5, accumulating facial damage can end a fight before a fighter is even knocked out or submitted. The doctor stoppage mechanic is part of the Real Impact System, and it gives the referee the ability to halt a match when a fighter’s injuries become too severe to continue safely. Understanding how the EA SPORTS UFC 5 doctor stoppage icon appears and escalates is the key to recognizing when a bout is at risk of being waved off by the cageside physician.

## How the EA SPORTS UFC 5 Doctor Stoppage Mechanic Works

The Real Impact System tracks cumulative damage, facial injuries, and health events to create dynamic fight outcomes. Unlike a knockout or submission, an EA SPORTS UFC 5 doctor stoppage is a fight-ending decision made by the cageside doctor after the referee pauses the action.

According to EA’s official tutorial, the Doctor Stoppage Icon results from facial injuries except mouth injuries. The more red the icon becomes, the closer a doctor is to stopping the fight. A [Real Impact System deep dive by The Sixth Axis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2023/09/20/ufc-5-gameplay-deep-dive-delves-into-the-real-impact-system/) confirms that doctor stoppages provide an alternative way for fights to end, separate from traditional finishes.

## Reading the Health and Stamina HUD Damage Icons

Damage icons represent injuries such as cuts and swelling, health events, and damage from submissions, and they appear in the Health and Stamina HUD. Tracking these icons is essential because they show exactly how much punishment a fighter has absorbed.

## Vision Penalty and Breathing Penalty Explained

Vision and breathing penalties are temporary debuffs, but they set the stage for larger health events. A vision penalty occurs when you take a cut or swelling on the brow, forehead, eye, or cheek. While active, it increases the damage you take on that side of the body.

A breathing penalty is triggered by facial injuries to the nose or mouth and reduces your current stamina recovery. Both can stack, and both are lifted if 30 seconds pass without further damage to that location or when a new round begins.

The EA SPORTS UFC 5 doctor stoppage is different. It is not a temporary penalty that resets between rounds. It represents accumulating facial trauma (excluding mouth injuries) that puts the fighter at risk of being ruled unable to continue.

## What Triggers the Doctor Stoppage Icon?

The Doctor Stoppage Icon does not appear from just any strike. EA states that it results from facial injuries except mouth injuries. The Sixth Axis adds that the icon first appears after a head injury other than a mouth injury.

It begins at a low possibility and increases as the injured area takes more damage. This means you must inflict repeated, targeted damage to the face—such as the brow, forehead, eye, or cheek—to escalate the icon toward a stoppage. Mouth injuries alone will not trigger the doctor stoppage icon, though they can cause a breathing penalty.

Because the icon escalates as the injured area absorbs more damage, the logical defensive adjustment is to protect the injured region and avoid absorbing additional strikes there.

## How a Fight Ends via Doctor Stoppage

When the Doctor Stoppage Icon becomes fully red, the risk threshold has been met. At this point, the referee halts the match and calls the doctor into the octagon to examine the fighter. The doctor then decides whether the fight can continue.

If the doctor rules the fighter cannot safely continue, the bout ends via doctor stoppage. This makes it one of the alternative conclusions to a fight in UFC 5, separate from a knockout, TKO, or submission. Watching the color of the icon is the clearest indicator that a fight is approaching this outcome.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What triggers an EA SPORTS UFC 5 doctor stoppage?

It is triggered by facial injuries except mouth injuries. The icon first appears after a head injury other than a mouth injury, begins at a low possibility, and increases with more damage to the injured area.

### Does a mouth injury cause a doctor stoppage?

No. EA’s official guide states the Doctor Stoppage Icon results from facial injuries except mouth injuries. However, a mouth injury can cause a breathing penalty, which decreases stamina recovery.

### How long do vision and breathing penalties last?

Both are lifted after 30 seconds without damage to the affected location or at the start of a new round. They can also stack if further damage is taken before they reset.

### Can a fight end without a knockout or submission?

Yes. Doctor stoppages provide an alternative way for fights to end. When the icon turns red, the referee halts the match, calls the doctor, and the doctor decides whether the fight can continue.
