## How Stamina Works in EA SPORTS UFC 5

In EA SPORTS UFC 5, stamina is not a single meter. The game tracks two connected values: Current Stamina and Maximum Stamina. Current Stamina is the energy you spend to act, while Maximum Stamina sets the ceiling for how much energy you can hold and recover. According to EA’s official [UFC 5 Stamina and Health Tutorial](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ufc/ufc-5/tips-and-tricks-hub/stamina-and-health-hub/stamina-hub), retrieved on November 2, 2026, every action drains Current Stamina. Because Current Stamina can only recover up to Maximum Stamina, your long-term endurance is effectively controlled by the size of the max pool.

## Current Stamina vs. Maximum Stamina

### The Light Gray Bar Behind Current Stamina

Maximum Stamina is displayed as a light gray bar behind the Current Stamina bar. If you watch only the front bar, you can miss the bigger problem: Maximum Stamina can shrink during a fight. When that happens, Current Stamina cannot recover past the reduced ceiling. Preventing Maximum Stamina from shrinking too far is therefore a core part of stamina management, because a smaller max pool means less total energy available down the stretch.

## Recovering Stamina During a Fight

Fighters are not doomed to drain out. The official tutorial states that fighters regain Stamina while inactive. The recovery is not uniform: you recover the most Stamina when standing still or when there is distance from the opponent. This makes movement and spacing strategic tools, not just offensive setups. If you keep acting, you keep draining Current Stamina, so disciplined pauses can protect your max pool.

### Standing Still and Keeping Distance

Use these verified recovery conditions:

- **Standing still:** The tutorial identifies standing still as one of the states that gives the greatest Stamina recovery while inactive.
- **Distance from the opponent:** Keeping distance also produces the strongest inactive recovery.
- **Constant action:** Every action drains Current Stamina, so continuous activity gives you no chance to recover energy.

## Between-Round Recovery and the Cardio Attribute

### What the Cardio Attribute Does

Between rounds, a fighter regains some Maximum Stamina. The amount regained depends on the fighter’s Cardio attribute, according to the official tutorial. Because the recovery is tied to Cardio, that attribute is central to how well a fighter regains a higher ceiling for later rounds.

For broader career context, EA’s [UFC 5 Beginner Tips news article](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ufc/ufc-5/news/ufc-5-beginner-tips) (published October 24, 2023) explains that you can create a fighter, choose a weight class and fighter type, and progress through Career Mode to level up stats. The beginner article highlights stand-up and submission skills among the stats you can improve, but it does not verify that Cardio or stamina values are upgradeable. Treat the Stamina tutorial’s Cardio rule as the confirmed mechanic for between-round recovery.

## Practical Stamina Management Takeaways

Based only on the verified first-party guidance, apply these steps:

- **Watch both bars:** Track Current Stamina for immediate energy and the light gray Maximum Stamina bar for your recovery ceiling.
- **Limit wasted actions:** Every action drains Current Stamina, so unnecessary output reduces the energy you have left to spend.
- **Recover with intent:** Stand still or keep distance when you need to refill, because those inactive states provide the greatest recovery.
- **Protect your ceiling:** Preventing Maximum Stamina from shrinking too far preserves the total Stamina you can regain.
- **Respect the between-round rule:** Maximum Stamina recovery between rounds depends on Cardio, so that attribute matters as fights go later.
