## How to Use Head Movement for EA SPORTS UFC 5 Striking Defense

Head movement is the core defensive tool in EA SPORTS UFC 5 striking defense. According to the official tutorial, players shift their fighter's head in various directions with the **right stick**. This technique enables a fighter to evade incoming strikes while staying close enough to immediately counterattack, rather than retreating out of range.

### Head Movement Combinations and Counters

The tutorial outlines specific pairings of defensive motion and offensive response:

- **Dodge to the right:** Immediately throw a **Left Hook** as a quick counter.
- **Lean or move the head back:** Use **Straights** to counter quickly.

EA’s tutorial notes that **Practice Mode** is the place to discover every possible head-movement strike combination. The two pairings above are the verified examples provided, and Practice Mode is where players can safely expand their defensive options.

## Strike Vulnerability: The Defensive Risk of Head Movement

Head movement is powerful, but it carries a major risk known as Strike Vulnerability. The [EA’s UFC 5 Strike Vulnerability: Defense tutorial](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ufc/ufc-5/tips-and-tricks-hub/striking-defense-hub/strike-vulnerability-defense-hub) warns that guiding your head into an opponent's attack will raise the damage you absorb.

Specific examples of dangerous mistakes include:

- **Ducking into an Uppercut:** Moving downward into a rising strike.
- **Swaying into a Roundhouse Kick:** Moving laterally into a looping kick.

Because of this, the tutorial explicitly cautions players not to become too predictable with head movement. If you repeatedly slip in the same direction, an opponent can bait you into moving directly into a damaging strike.

## Putting EA SPORTS UFC 5 Striking Defense Into Practice

To build reliable defense, use the tools the game gives you:

1. **Enter Practice Mode** to safely learn head-movement timing and discover all possible strike combinations.
2. **Use the right stick** to slip strikes while staying in range, rather than relying on backing away.
3. **Master the verified counters:** throw a Left Hook after moving your head right, and Straights after moving your head back.
4. **Vary your movement** to avoid strike vulnerability. Mix up directions and rhythms so you are not ducking into uppercuts or swaying into roundhouse kicks.

These steps are directly supported by EA’s official tutorials. There is no need to guess at advanced defensive schemes—focus on right-stick head movement, the two confirmed counter combinations, and managing vulnerability.
