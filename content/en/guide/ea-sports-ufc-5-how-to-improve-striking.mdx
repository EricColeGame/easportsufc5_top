## How to Improve Striking in EA SPORTS UFC 5

Winning fights in EA SPORTS UFC 5 starts with sharp, well-timed striking, so knowing how to improve that skill directly affects your results in the Octagon. The verified way to get better is to use the official EA SPORTS UFC 5 Striking Offense Tips and Tricks hub to study its listed training sessions and to progress through Career Mode to raise your stand-up game stats. ## Use the Official Striking Offense Tips and Tricks Hub

EA’s official Tips and Tricks site includes a dedicated Striking Offense hub. The page organizes striking help into broad categories: Punches, Kicks, Spinning Strikes, Strike Vulnerability, Clinch Offense, Combinations, Simplified Controls, and Running Strikes.

These entries show the game provides structured practice scenarios for the core tools of stand-up offense. For the full official list and artwork, see the [EA SPORTS UFC 5 Striking Offense Tips and Tricks hub](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ufc/ufc-5/tips-and-tricks-hub/striking-offense-hub).

## Level Up Your Stand-Up Game in Career Mode

Improving your fighter’s striking attributes is tied to Career Mode progression, not just to match results. EA’s UFC 5 Beginner Tips article, published October 24, 2023, explains that after you create a fighter, choose a weight class, and select a fighter type, you can level up stats as you progress through Career Mode. Those stats cover everything from your stand-up game to your submission skills, so raising the stand-up portion is the confirmed way to strengthen striking.

To create the fighter whose stand-up you will develop, the article notes you can customize appearance details such as hair, eye color, and tattoos before jumping into Career Mode. The key takeaway is consistent play through Career Mode, where the level-up system lets you invest in the stand-up game.

## Punch Card XP and Rewards: What Actually Carries Over

It is important to distinguish Punch Card progression from fighter stat progression. The Beginner Tips article states that you earn XP by playing any game mode and that completing challenges on the challenge board grants a solid amount of XP at once. Different XP amounts are awarded for online and offline matches, so participating in both is encouraged.

That XP levels up your Punch Card, and requirements can vary from level to level and from week to week; Punch Card rewards also differ and expire. UFC Coins earned through Punch Card rewards and Fight Week contracts can be spent at the Store on gear, accessories, emotes, playercard customizations, and sometimes Alter Egos. The official material does not confirm that any of those purchases increase striking power, so view them as customization and collection rewards rather than a stat shortcut.
