## Step 1: Single-Player Career Mode and the GOAT Status

Your first major goal is to start a Solo Career from the title screen and create a fighter with the end goal of becoming the GOAT (Greatest of All Time). After a few early wins you will join the UFC, and the Career title screen will display a “GOAT” menu showing your overall progress.

You do not need to finish every requirement. The guide confirms you only need any **6 of 10 Performance Records** and any **2 of 4 Promotion Records**. The recommended focus is:

- **Performance:** 17 submissions, 21 finishes, 26 UFC wins, 17 consecutive wins, 12 consecutive UFC title defenses, and 1 Two Division Champion.
- **Promotion:** 8 million followers and 19 PPV main events.

### Fastest GOAT Method

Play on Easy and always choose the short 1-week training camp instead of the long 5–6 week version. Skip sparring sessions, run promotions to build fans and hype, and simulate to the fight. Then submit or knock out the opponent in the first round, which usually takes about 30 seconds. If you are about to lose, close the game before it saves to protect your win streak, and consider backing up your save to PS Plus Cloud.

Important: stay connected to PSN and EA servers with an EA account linked to your PSN. Accolades and profile progress, including the five Accolades needed for the “I Did That” trophy, cannot be earned offline.

## Step 2: Online Trophies and Multiplayer Requirements

Roughly half the list requires online play. You must be connected to the servers, and several trophies are tied to specific modes.

### Online Career and the Championship Belt

Create a fighter in Online Career and simply finish one match to start a season and unlock the first online trophy. Spend Evolution Points to upgrade a fighter; after you put 1,000 Evolution Points into one fighter, you can **prestige** that fighter to earn the “Pound for Pound” gold trophy.

The tougher challenge is “And Still.” You need to win 3 matches in a row in either Online Career or Ranked Championships to earn a Championship Belt. Then you must successfully defend the title by winning one more match.

### Blitz Battles

From the online menu, join a Blitz Battle and finish a single match to unlock the entry trophy. To reach Round 3, you need 2 consecutive wins; you do not have to win Round 3 itself. Blitz rules rotate every 24 hours, and you can rejoin after a loss to retry.

### Legendary Item and Fight Week

For “Fine Collection,” go to the title screen Store and buy one Legendary (orange) cosmetic using in-game gold coins or as a Fight Week reward. The trophy does not pop at purchase; open your Online Career fighter’s “Edit Appearance” menu to unlock it.

Fight Week is a live-service mode that only appears during the week of real-world UFC PPV events. During that window, unlock a challenge, set your Picks, and complete all available challenges (usually 10) to finish the Fight Week trophies.

## Notable Trophies and Combat Challenges

Most combat trophies can be earned in any mode, and Easy difficulty makes them painless.

- **Relentless:** Finish a fight with ground and pound by striking a downed opponent with square or triangle.
- **You Either Win or Learn / Joint Venture:** Win by a choke submission (guillotine, rear-naked choke, D’arce) or a joint submission (armbar, kimura). Takedowns followed by ground transitions make this straightforward with Jiu-Jitsu specialists.
- **Just Bleed:** Take enough head damage that the medkit injury icon turns red, giving a chance for a doctor to enter the cage. Fighting the CPU on Legendary and surviving increases the odds.
- **Let me Tell You a Secret:** Initiate a clinch and strike repeatedly until the opponent is knocked out while still clinched.
- **Block This:** Use a kick with the “Guard Break” icon in the movelist to break an opponent’s block.
- **Shape Shifter:** Acquire an Alter Ego Fighter. Digital Standard includes Volk and Shevchenko, while Deluxe adds Mike Tyson, Fedor Emelianenko, Muhammad Ali, and Bruce Lee. New Alter Egos can also come from Fight Week.

## Boosting and Online Matchmaking Tips

The online trophies are semi-boostable, but the game has strict matchmaking prevention. You can only fight the same account once per mode, so serious boosting requires multiple prepared accounts.

- Search only one weight class at the same time as your boosting partner, ideally during quiet hours (around 5–7 AM ET).
- Prepare at least four new PSN accounts, each linked to an EA account, because you will not rematch the same player again in that mode.
- Blitz Battles are easier to boost because you only need 2 straight wins, and round matching can sometimes pull new players forward.

If you struggle with online wins, practice against the AI on Legendary first. Submissions are the most reliable tactic because many lower-ranked players have trouble defending them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many hours does the EA SPORTS UFC 5 trophy guide take to platinum?

This EA SPORTS UFC 5 trophy guide estimates 10–15 hours to platinum, with an overall difficulty of 4/10. Single-player trophies are closer to 2/10, while online trophies are somewhat skill-dependent but semi-boostable.

### Does difficulty affect any UFC 5 trophies?

No, difficulty does not affect trophies. You can play on Easy and still unlock every offline and combat trophy, including quick first-round finishes. Online trophies still require real wins against other players.

### Are there any glitched or missable trophies?

The roadmap reports 0 glitched trophies. However, the GOAT career trophy can become missable if your fighter’s longevity runs out before you finish the required records, and Fight Week trophies are only available during real-world UFC PPV weeks. Accolades for “I Did That” also will not unlock if you play Career offline without a linked EA account.

### Do I need to play online to get the platinum?

Yes, 14 of the trophies are online, including winning a Championship Belt and reaching Round 3 in Blitz Battles. You must be connected to PSN and EA servers with a linked EA account for profile progress and cosmetic unlocks.
