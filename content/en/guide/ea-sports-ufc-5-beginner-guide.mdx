Jumping into EA SPORTS UFC 5 for the first time is exciting, but new players often miss out on early rewards because they do not know how progression and currency actually work. This EA SPORTS UFC 5 beginner guide gives you the concrete path: earn XP to fill your Punch Card, collect UFC Coins through Punch Card rewards and Fight Week contracts, and create a custom fighter to develop in Career Mode. Using only verified first-party information from [EA’s official beginner tips](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ufc/ufc-5/news/ufc-5-beginner-tips) (published October 24, 2023), you will learn the exact limits—like the fact that Punch Cards expire and level-up requirements can change week to week—so you can play smarter from day one.

## How the Punch Card System Works

Your Punch Card is the core progression track in EA SPORTS UFC 5. As you play, the experience points (XP) you earn grant level progress, and new rewards unlock at each level.

### Earning XP in Online and Offline Matches

You can earn XP by playing any of the different game modes available in the game. However, not all matches are valued equally.

- Online and offline matches award different amounts of XP, so you should participate in both to maximize progress.
- A hot tip from EA is to complete the challenges on the challenge board, because doing so will grant a solid amount of XP in one go.

### Understanding Weekly Punch Cards and Expiration

Punch Cards are not permanent, and their structure can change.

- The amount of XP required to level up can vary from level to level.
- Week 2’s Punch Card may level up differently than Week 1’s Punch Card did.
- Rewards differ from Punch Card to Punch Card, and each card expires, so be sure to complete them before they are gone.

## Earning and Spending UFC Coins

UFC Coins act as a spendable currency that lets you personalize your experience. EA confirms there are a number of ways to earn them, but specifically highlights the following methods.

### How to Earn UFC Coins

- **Punch Card rewards:** Progressing your Punch Card can grant coins.
- **Fight Week contracts:** Taking part in Fight Week contracts is another way to build your balance.

### What to Buy with UFC Coins

Once you have enough coins, head to the Store to spend them.

- Buy new gear, accessories, or emotes for your fighter.
- Buy playercard customizations, including new profile pictures or backgrounds, to customize your profile instead of your fighter.
- On occasion, Alter Egos can also be bought with coins.

The goal is to unlock challenges, earn coins, and fully customize your created fighter however you want.

## Creating Your Fighter from Scratch

EA SPORTS UFC 5 allows you to develop and customize your very own fighter from scratch, giving you control over both appearance and competitive identity.

### Appearance Customization

You can create a fighter that looks however you want. The game lets you adjust many appearance details, including:

- Hair style
- Eye color
- A variety of tattoos

### Weight Class and Fighter Type

After finalizing your look, you must make two key gameplay decisions:

- Choose the weight class your fighter will compete in.
- Then, select the fighter type that matches your preferred approach.

## Progressing Through Career Mode

Creating a fighter is only the beginning. Career Mode is where you begin as an unknown amateur and work toward UFC superstar status.

As you progress through Career Mode, you can level up your stats. The areas you can improve range from your stand-up game to your submission skills. The ultimate goal is to climb the ranks and become the next UFC World Champion.

## EA SPORTS UFC 5 Beginner Guide FAQ

### What is the fastest way to earn XP for the Punch Card in EA SPORTS UFC 5?

According to EA, completing challenges on the challenge board will grant a solid amount of XP in one go. You should also play both online and offline matches, since they award different amounts of XP and both count toward your Punch Card level progress.

### How do I get UFC Coins in EA SPORTS UFC 5?

EA states that UFC Coins can be earned through Punch Card rewards and Fight Week contracts. After earning coins, you can spend them at the Store on fighter gear, accessories, emotes, playercard customizations, and sometimes Alter Egos.

### Do Punch Cards in EA SPORTS UFC 5 expire?

Yes. EA warns that Punch Cards expire, so you need to complete them before they are gone. Additionally, the XP required to level up and the rewards offered can differ from one weekly Punch Card to the next.

### Can I customize my own fighter in EA SPORTS UFC 5?

Yes. You can build a fighter from scratch and adjust appearance details like hair, eye color, and tattoos. You will also pick a weight class and fighter type, then develop stats such as stand-up and submission skills in Career Mode.
