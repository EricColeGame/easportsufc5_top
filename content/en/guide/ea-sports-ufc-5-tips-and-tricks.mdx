## EA SPORTS UFC 5 Tips and Tricks: Getting Started

Stepping into the Octagon for the first time can feel overwhelming, but the right EA SPORTS UFC 5 tips and tricks make early progress far less intimidating. Understanding how Punch Card XP, UFC Coins, and Career Mode creation work helps you unlock rewards faster and build a fighter that matches your style. Based on EA’s official beginner guide published October 24, 2023, the proven path is to earn XP across game modes for Punch Card rewards, spend UFC Coins in the Store, and develop a custom fighter—while remembering that Punch Card level requirements and rewards can change from week to week.

All of the guidance below comes from the official [EA SPORTS UFC 5 Beginner Tips article on EA.com](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ufc/ufc-5/news/ufc-5-beginner-tips), which EA published on October 24, 2023. It remains the clearest first-party overview of these systems, so treat it as the source of truth for the mechanics described here.

## Punch Card Progression: Earn XP and Unlock Rewards

Your Punch Card is the main track for turning playtime into unlockables. You earn experience points (XP) by playing any of the different game modes, and that XP grants level progress on your Punch Card. As you gain levels, new rewards unlock.

- **Play across modes:** Any game mode you play can help you earn XP, so do not limit yourself to a single mode.
- **Use the challenge board:** Completing challenges on the challenge board can award a solid amount of XP at one time, making it a strong early boost.
- **Mix online and offline:** Online and offline matches grant different amounts of XP, so participate in both to keep progression steady.
- **Expect variation and expiration:** The XP required to level up can vary from level to level. A Week 2 Punch Card can level up differently than a Week 1 card, and rewards differ from Punch Card to Punch Card. Complete each one before it expires.

UFC Coins are the currency you will use to customize your experience. There are a number of ways to earn them, including Punch Card rewards and Fight Week contracts.

Because the official guide says there are a number of ways to earn coins, treat those earning methods as examples rather than the only options. Once you have enough coins, spend them at the Store to buy new gear, accessories, or emotes for your created fighter. If you would rather customize your profile than your fighter, you can also purchase player card customizations like new profile pics or backgrounds. Sometimes Alter Egos are available to buy with coins as well.

## How to Create a Fighter and Progress in Career Mode

Creation starts with appearance and leads into long-term progression. In EA SPORTS UFC 5, you can develop and customize your own fighter from scratch, then take that fighter into Career Mode.

- **Design the look:** Customize everything from hair and eye color to different types of tattoos.
- **Choose the fundamentals:** Pick a weight class and select your fighter type before moving forward.
- **Level up in Career Mode:** As you progress, you can level up stats across areas such as your stand-up game and submission skills.
- **Chase championship glory:** The Career Mode journey is framed as going from an unknown amateur to a UFC superstar, with the goal of becoming UFC World Champion.

The fighter you create is also the one you can outfit with Store purchases, so your creation choices carry into how you use UFC Coins later.

## EA SPORTS UFC 5 Tips and Tricks FAQ

### What are the most useful EA SPORTS UFC 5 tips and tricks for beginners?

The most useful EA SPORTS UFC 5 tips and tricks for beginners are to earn XP in any mode, complete challenge board challenges for a large XP batch, and play both online and offline matches because XP payouts differ. From there, focus on Punch Card rewards, UFC Coins, and building your Career Mode fighter before each Punch Card expires.

### How do I earn and spend UFC Coins in EA SPORTS UFC 5?

You can earn UFC Coins through Punch Card rewards and Fight Week contracts, among other methods. Spend them in the Store on fighter gear, accessories, emotes, player card customizations like profile pics or backgrounds, and sometimes Alter Egos.

### Can I customize my fighter before playing Career Mode?

Yes. Create a fighter from scratch and customize appearance details such as hair, eye color, and tattoos. Then pick a weight class and fighter type. In Career Mode, you can level up stats like stand-up and submission skills as you chase a UFC World Championship.

### Do Punch Cards change over time?

Yes. The XP required to level up can vary from level to level, and a Week 2 Punch Card can level differently than a Week 1 card. Rewards also differ from Punch Card to Punch Card, so complete each one before it expires.
