Climbing the online ranks in EA SPORTS UFC 5 takes more than button-mashing; it requires smart practice and steady fighter progression. Without a plan, new players can stall out in Career Mode or miss the training tools that fix bad habits. The fastest verified path to improvement is to use the official Tips and Tricks hubs for targeted striking and grappling drills, while earning XP and leveling your Punch Card to unlock rewards and upgrade your created fighter’s stand-up and submission stats.

The Striking Offense section further organizes training into Punches, Kicks, Spinning Strikes, Strike Vulnerability, Clinch Offense, Combinations, Simplified Controls, and Running Strikes. ## Train Every Phase: Defense, Ground, and Stamina

Becoming well-rounded means not stopping at striking. The same Tips and Tricks hub includes Striking Defense, Ground Offense, Ground Defense, Stamina & Health, and Submissions. Working through those categories helps you avoid becoming one-dimensional. The official hub lists these sections, but as with striking, the expanded text is restricted by the age gate.

## Build a Better Fighter in Career Mode

Your created fighter’s development is central to long-term improvement. In EA SPORTS UFC 5, you can build a fighter from scratch, customizing appearance details like hair, eye color, and tattoos. After that, you pick a weight class and a fighter type.

As you advance through Career Mode, you can level up attributes across the board, from your stand-up game to your submission skills. This comes from EA’s [UFC 5 Beginner Tips news article](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ufc/ufc-5/news/ufc-5-beginner-tips), published October 24, 2023.

## Earn XP and Complete Punch Cards to Unlock Rewards

Progression systems also support your growth. You earn XP by playing any game mode, and completing challenges on the challenge board grants a solid amount of XP at once. EA’s official beginner tips note that online and offline matches award different XP amounts, so it is worth participating in both.

XP fills your Punch Card, unlocking rewards as you level up. Keep in mind that XP requirements can change from level to level. The 2023 article used Week 1 and Week 2 punch cards as examples of how rewards and leveling can differ; punch cards expire, so complete them before they rotate out.

## Use UFC Coins to Customize Your Fighter and Profile

UFC Coins are earned through Punch Card rewards and Fight Week contracts. You can spend them in the Store on gear, accessories, and emotes for your fighter, or on playercard customizations such as profile pictures and backgrounds.

EA’s tips also state that Alter Egos are occasionally available for coins. These items let you personalize your identity, but the verified material does not describe them as direct stat boosts.

## EA SPORTS UFC 5 How to Get Better: FAQ

### What is the verified EA SPORTS UFC 5 how to get better method for beginners?

The verified method is to train in the official Tips and Tricks hubs, especially Striking Offense drills, then build your fighter in Career Mode and progress the Punch Card to unlock rewards. Practice striking first, then expand into defense, ground, and stamina training.

### How do I improve striking faster in EA SPORTS UFC 5?

Run the Striking Offense training sessions listed on the official hub. These cover jabs, straights, hooks, overhands, uppercuts, roundhouse kicks, leg kicks, cage kicks, spinning attacks, and clinch attempts. Repetition in these categories builds familiarity.

### Do online matches give more XP than offline in EA SPORTS UFC 5?

EA’s official beginner tips confirm that online and offline matches award different amounts of XP, but they do not state which is higher. To maximize Punch Card progress, participate in both.

### Can I raise my fighter’s submission and stand-up skills?

Yes. Career Mode lets you level up your created fighter’s stats over time, including both stand-up and submission skills, after you choose a weight class and fighter type.
