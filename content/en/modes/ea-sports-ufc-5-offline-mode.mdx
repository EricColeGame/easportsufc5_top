If you’re searching for an EA SPORTS UFC 5 offline mode, the short answer is that the game does not use a single menu option with that exact name in EA’s official materials, but it clearly supports offline matches and single-player play. Offline matches are confirmed to award XP, and Career Mode, Practice Mode, and solo fights versus AI let you play without jumping into online competition. That matters because your Punch Card progression, fighter development, and rewards do not have to depend entirely on online matches.

## What “EA SPORTS UFC 5 offline mode” Really Means

When players ask about an EA SPORTS UFC 5 offline mode, they usually mean fighting the CPU without playing other people, or earning progress outside of online matches. EA’s own Beginner Tips page uses the term “offline matches” directly, noting that online and offline matches grant different amounts of XP.

The official Accessibility Resources page says you can “play as pro-fighters in solo fights or online against friends,” and Wikipedia lists the game’s modes as Single-player and multiplayer. Put simply, offline play is supported, but it is delivered through matches and modes rather than one tile called “Offline Mode.”

## Offline Matches and Punch Card Progression

Your Punch Card is the main progression track mentioned in EA’s Beginner Tips guide. You earn experience points, or XP, by playing any of the different game modes, and that XP grants level progress that unlocks rewards.

Most importantly for offline players, EA states that different amounts of XP are given for online and offline matches, so you are encouraged to participate in both. The [official EA SPORTS UFC 5 Beginner Tips page](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ufc/ufc-5/news/ufc-5-beginner-tips) also notes that completing challenges on the challenge board can net a solid amount of XP at once.

Keep these Punch Card limits in mind:

- Weekly Punch Cards can change: Week 2’s Punch Card may level up differently than Week 1’s did.
- Rewards differ from Punch Card to Punch Card.
- You should complete Punch Cards before they expire.

## Single-Player and Solo Modes You Can Play Offline

### Career Mode (Offline Single-Player)

Career Mode is the main single-player career experience. You create a fighter from scratch, pick a weight class, and select a fighter type. As you progress, you can level up stats ranging from your stand-up game to your submission skills, with the goal of going from unknown amateur to UFC superstar.

When you create a fighter, you choose one of five fighting styles:

- Kickboxer
- Boxer
- MMA
- Wrestler
- Jiu-jitsu

Each style has its own strengths and weaknesses, so check your fighter’s perks and ratings before fighting. EA Help presents Career Mode as separate from Online Career Mode, and Wikipedia notes that Coach Davis returns to guide you toward becoming the “G.O.A.T.”

### Fight Contract Matches and Live Fight Week

Fight Week is an event-driven live-service mode, not an offline campaign. EA Help says it lets you take your fighter through challenges that sync up with real-world UFC Pay-Per-View events, and the Game Modes Deep Dive describes it as an event-driven live service. Treat it as a connected feature rather than part of your offline rotation.

Wikipedia’s launch-era overview, published October 27, 2023, separately describes a “fight contract mode” that generates a match with AI-controlled fighters on a daily basis. The source does not state whether Fight Contracts require an online connection, so do not assume they are offline until the game itself confirms that.

### Practice Mode and Training Manual

If you want to sharpen your skills offline, the Learn tab in the Main Menu holds the Training Manual and Practice Mode. The Training Manual explains the controls for essential moves, and Practice Mode lets you master those skills.

EA Help recommends both for new and returning players, and neither description mentions an online requirement. That makes them a natural fit for offline training before you take on AI opponents.

## Customizing Offline Difficulty and Controls

Offline play against AI can be tuned through several options. The Difficulty setting changes how challenging AI opponents are, so you can make fights easier or harder depending on your skill level.

EA’s Accessibility Resources page highlights a few useful settings:

- **Simple Controls:** This lets the AI choose the best available strikes, combinations, and actions against your opponent. EA notes this setting is not available in Online Modes, which makes it an offline-friendly option.
- **Grapple Assist:** This simplifies offensive grappling by letting the AI determine directions while you use the left stick for grappling moves.
- **Defensive Grapple Assist:** This does the same thing for defensive grappling.

EA warns that using these assists can be a disadvantage in online competitive fights against players who use Hybrid or Legacy Grapple HUD, so they are best viewed as offline or casual tools.

## Platforms and Availability for Offline Play

UFC 5 is available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, according to EA Help and the EA SPORTS UFC 5 site. Because those are the only listed platforms, offline matches and single-player modes are played there.

It is also the first game in the franchise to carry a Mature (M) rating. EA Help notes this means content may not be suitable for players under 17, partly due to the realistic injury system and stronger language in the career mode, so keep that in mind for younger players.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there an EA SPORTS UFC 5 offline mode?

There is no official mode labeled only as an “EA SPORTS UFC 5 offline mode” in EA’s materials. Offline play is supported through offline matches, solo fights, Career Mode, and Practice Mode rather than one separate offline menu.

### Do offline matches in EA SPORTS UFC 5 award XP?

Yes. EA’s Beginner Tips guide confirms that online and offline matches give different amounts of XP, and both can help you level up your Punch Card. Completing challenge board challenges can also grant a solid amount of XP at once.

### Can I play Career Mode without playing online?

Yes. Career Mode is the single-player career guided by Coach Davis, and EA Help lists it separately from Online Career Mode. You create a fighter, pick a weight class and one of five fighting styles, and level up stats as you chase the G.O.A.T. goal.

### Is Practice Mode available offline in EA SPORTS UFC 5?

Practice Mode and the Training Manual are found in the Learn tab, and EA Help recommends them for learning controls and mastering moves. EA’s description does not state an online requirement, making them a sensible training option to test in your own build before an online match.
