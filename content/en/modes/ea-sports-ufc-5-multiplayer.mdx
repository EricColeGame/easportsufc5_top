## What EA SPORTS UFC 5 Multiplayer Includes

If you are stepping into the Octagon with friends or rivals around the world, EA SPORTS UFC 5 multiplayer turns every match into a high-stakes test of striking and grappling. This matters because the game’s online modes change how you build a fighter, manage stamina, and adjust assists before you ever throw a punch. Based on official EA and verified reference material, the confirmed multiplayer centerpiece is Online Career Mode, where you create a custom fighter and battle other players’ created characters with matches made by skill level, advancement through divisions, and title opportunities in four divisions.

The base game launched on October 27, 2023, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, and its listed modes include both single-player and multiplayer. EA’s accessibility resources confirm you can play as pro fighters in solo fights or online against friends. Among the game’s connected modes, Online Career Mode is the clearly verified competitive online experience, while Fight Week delivers live UFC-themed challenges.

## Online Career Mode: The Core Multiplayer Experience

Online Career Mode lets you build a custom fighter and climb toward fame by competing online. According to reference coverage of the game, this is an online career experience in which your own created character faces other characters made by other players. EA’s official site adds that the mode uses skill-based matchmaking, supports division progression, and offers title chases across four divisions.

That structure means your online journey is tied to a persistent custom career rather than a single standalone exhibition bout. You are not just jumping into one fight; you are developing a fighter through divisional ranks and championship opportunities.

### Creating Your Custom Fighter

Before competing online, you create a custom Fighter. EA’s help guide says you choose between five fighting styles:

- Kickboxer
- Boxer
- MMA
- Wrestler
- Jiu-jitsu

Each style has its own strengths and weaknesses, so review your Fighter’s perks and ratings before entering a match. The same guide recommends using the Training Manual and Practice Mode in the Learn tab to master essential controls. It also warns against mashing buttons during fights, because learning accurate inputs delivers better results and preserves your Fighter’s energy.

### Skill-Based Matchmaking and Progression

EA’s official site states that Online Career Mode brings skill-based matchmaking, division progression, and title chases across four divisions. In practical terms, the game tries to pair you with opponents of comparable skill as you pursue championship runs. Your custom character’s online career advances through divisional competition rather than resetting after every fight.

## Fight Week and Real-World UFC Events

Fight Week is another highlighted mode in EA SPORTS UFC 5. The official help article explains that you take your Fighter through challenges that sync up with real-world UFC Pay-Per-View events. EA’s site also notes that Fight Week content, features, and challenges are updated monthly around real-world UFC events.

The provided sources describe Fight Week as a connected content experience, but they do not explicitly confirm it as a direct head-to-head multiplayer mode. Players looking for verified competitive matches against other people should prioritize Online Career Mode.

## Multiplayer Settings, Assists, and Accessibility

Not every control setting behaves the same once you go online. EA’s accessibility resources state that Simple Controls—which let the AI choose strikes, combinations, and actions—are not available in Online Modes. If you want a simplified grappling option, the game offers Grapple and Defensive Grapple assists.

However, EA warns that using these assists in online competitive fights may pose a challenge when facing opponents who use Hybrid or Legacy Grapple HUD during grappling. Other accessibility tools, such as colorblind filters, locked camera side positioning, contrast, brightness, and subtitles, are available across consoles for the English, US version of the game. These options help tailor presentation and clarity, even though they do not directly change matchmaking or rules.

## How to Start Competing in EA SPORTS UFC 5 Multiplayer

Use these supported steps to begin online competition:

- Launch EA SPORTS UFC 5 on PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S.
- Create a custom Fighter and select one of the five fighting styles that fits your strategy.
- Open the Learn tab to review the Training Manual and practice in Practice Mode before playing online.
- Enter Online Career Mode to challenge players worldwide; your created character will face other players’ created characters.
- Check your control settings before fighting online, and remember that Simple Controls are disabled in Online Modes.

For the full first-party overview of platforms, modes, and related setup details, see [EA’s official getting started guide for UFC 5](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/ufc/ufc-5/intro-to-ufc-5/).
