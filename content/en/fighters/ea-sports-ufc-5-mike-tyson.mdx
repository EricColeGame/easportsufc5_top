## EA SPORTS UFC 5 Mike Tyson: Unlocking the Legendary KO King

Few names in combat sports carry the raw intimidation of “Iron” Mike Tyson, and EA SPORTS UFC 5 lets you bring that fearsome legacy into the Octagon. For MMA fans and boxing purists alike, unlocking Tyson matters because it adds a pure knockout artist to a game built around realistic damage and strategy. You can add the EA SPORTS UFC 5 Mike Tyson fighter by buying the standalone add-on or purchasing a Deluxe or Ultimate edition that bundles him, but the base game is always required.

The add-on launched on October 23, 2023, a few days before the full game’s October 27, 2023 release, and it is published by Electronic Arts. According to the [official PlayStation Store listing for UFC 5 - Mike Tyson](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/UP0006-PPSA03540_00-MIKETYSON0000000), the content requires EA SPORTS UFC 5 (sold separately), all game updates, an internet connection, and an EA Account. Some items may also unlock through gameplay and can only be earned once, so players who already unlocked content via gameplay will not receive it again.

## What Is the EA SPORTS UFC 5 Mike Tyson Add-On?

The EA SPORTS UFC 5 Mike Tyson add-on is a paid character pack that introduces the legendary heavyweight boxer as a playable fighter. Priced at $4.19 on both PlayStation and Xbox stores, PlayStation users can grab it for $3.77 with an EA Play membership. The pack carries an ESRB Mature 17+ rating for blood, strong language, and violence, and it supports optional in-game purchases and online play.

On PlayStation, the add-on holds a 3.99 out of 5-star rating based on 788 player ratings. The short description calls Tyson a “fearsome phenom who captured the imagination of an era,” inviting players to “stand, bang, and make the Octagon” theirs with the legendary KO king. It is also included in the UFC 5 Ultimate Edition Upgrade and the full Ultimate Edition on Xbox, making those purchase paths an alternative to buying the standalone pack.

## How Mike Tyson Fits Into UFC 5 Gameplay and Damage System

EA SPORTS UFC 5 is built on the Frostbite engine and introduces an all-new authentic damage system that changes how fighters perform. Cuts, bruising, and swelling accumulate based on strikes landed and taken, with over 64,000 possible combinations of facial damage. Because Tyson is known as a legendary KO king, his inclusion complements a system where heavy damage can affect mobility, defense, and stamina. If a fighter sustains a serious injury like a broken nose or swollen eye, the referee can pause the action so a ringside doctor can check the damage, and the match can even be halted.

The game also streamlined grappling with faster submissions and removed mini-games, while adding new strikes and hit reactions for ground-and-pound elbows and spinning attacks. Tyson’s boxing-centric power fits naturally into the revamped striking animations that emulate heavy hitters. Beyond exhibition matches, UFC 5 features a career mode and an online career mode, though the provided material does not explicitly confirm whether legends like Tyson are usable in those specific modes.

## Player Reception and Ratings for the Mike Tyson Content

Fan response to the base game and the Mike Tyson add-on has been mixed to positive depending on the platform. Community feedback on the base game, however, includes sharp criticism. Another reviewer, NiGhTMonKeY-2099 (April 7, 2026), simply asked why they downloaded such a “ridiculously bad” game. These are community opinions from the [PlayStation Store user reviews for EA SPORTS UFC 5](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/ea-sports-ufc-5/?srsltid=AYCX9vJSlD-fQLqx_JmPqTFHK8MPSGK-73KlivIFN1aFKtJeizWgrPuW) and do not reflect official EA statements. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does the EA SPORTS UFC 5 Mike Tyson add-on cost?**  
The EA SPORTS UFC 5 Mike Tyson add-on is priced at $4.19. PlayStation players with an EA Play membership can purchase it for $3.77. Remember that the base game is required and sold separately.

**Can I get Mike Tyson in the Standard Edition of UFC 5?**  
No. The Standard Edition ($69.99) only includes the base game. To get Mike Tyson without buying the standalone pack, you need the Deluxe Edition ($79.99) or Ultimate Edition ($99.99), both of which list Mike Tyson as included content on the PlayStation Store.

**When was the EA SPORTS UFC 5 Mike Tyson content released?**  
The standalone Mike Tyson add-on released on October 23, 2023. This was a few days before the main EA SPORTS UFC 5 game launched on October 27, 2023, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

**Is Mike Tyson playable in UFC 5 Career Mode?**  
The provided reference material does not confirm whether Mike Tyson is playable in Career Mode. The add-on description only states that players can “make the Octagon” theirs with Tyson, and the game’s career mode details focus on created fighters and online progression rather than legendary boxers.
