## How to Build a Top Fighter in Career Mode

If you prefer to create your own elite fighter, EA SPORTS UFC 5 lets you develop a custom athlete from scratch. You can customize appearance details such as hair, eye color, and tattoos, then choose a weight class and fighter type. As you progress through Career Mode, you can level up key stats, including stand-up and submission skills, by earning XP. Playing different modes and completing challenge-board challenges grants XP for Punch Card progression. You can also earn UFC Coins through Punch Card rewards and Fight Week contracts, then spend them in the Store on gear, accessories, emotes, and select Alter Egos.
