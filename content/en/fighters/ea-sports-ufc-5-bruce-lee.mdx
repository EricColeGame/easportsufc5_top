Bruce Lee returns to the virtual Octagon in EA SPORTS UFC 5, letting players fight as the father of mixed martial arts across multiple weight classes. If you want to add the EA SPORTS UFC 5 Bruce Lee content to your roster, you will need to look at the dedicated Bruce Lee Bundle or individual Alter Ego releases on Xbox. This guide explains exactly what the Bruce Lee content includes, its requirements, and how in-game progression like UFC Coins and Punch Cards work.

## What’s Included in the EA SPORTS UFC 5 Bruce Lee Content?

The UFC 5 Bruce Lee Bundle brings the martial arts legend into the game as a playable fighter through brand-new Alter Egos. According to the official Xbox store listing, this bundle lets you fight with Bruce Lee across four divisions: Bantamweight, Featherweight, Lightweight, and Welterweight.

Inside the bundle, you will find the Bruce Lee Pre-Order Bundle and the Bruce Lee – Welterweight add-on. The listing confirms the content is published by Electronic Arts and developed by EA SPORTS, with a release date of October 23, 2023. The bundle is priced at $9.99, though EA Play members can save $1.00.

Some items may also be available through gameplay, but they can only be unlocked once. If you have already unlocked an item via gameplay, the bundle will not grant it to you again.

## How to Use Bruce Lee Alter Egos and Create a Fighter

In EA SPORTS UFC 5, an Alter Ego gives you a unique version of a fighter to take into the Octagon. The Bruce Lee – Welterweight description invites players to take the father of mixed martial arts from cultural icon to UFC champion with this new Alter Ego.

If you also want to build your own legend, the game offers a deep Create a Fighter system. You can customize your fighter’s appearance, including hair, eye color, and tattoos. After finalizing the look, you pick a weight class and select a fighter type. As you progress through Career Mode, you can level up important stats such as your stand-up game and submission skills, going from an unknown amateur to a UFC World Champion.

## Earning Rewards: Punch Cards, XP, and UFC Coins

Progression in EA SPORTS UFC 5 relies heavily on Punch Cards and UFC Coins, which are explained in EA’s official beginner tips.

### Leveling Up Your Punch Card

You earn experience points (XP) by playing any of the different game modes. Completing challenges on the challenge board is a great way to earn a solid amount of XP at once. Keep in mind:

- Online and offline matches grant different amounts of XP.
- The XP required to level up can vary from level to level.
- Punch Cards expire, and rewards differ from one Punch Card to the next, so complete them before time runs out.

### Spending UFC Coins

You can earn UFC Coins through Punch Card rewards and Fight Week contracts. Once you have enough, spend them at the Store to buy gear, accessories, or emotes for your fighter. Coins can also purchase playercard customizations like profile pictures and backgrounds. Notably, EA notes that Alter Egos can sometimes be bought with UFC Coins, giving you another way to expand your roster.

For more on earning XP and coins, check out the official [EA SPORTS UFC 5 Beginner Tips](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ufc/ufc-5/news/ufc-5-beginner-tips) article.
