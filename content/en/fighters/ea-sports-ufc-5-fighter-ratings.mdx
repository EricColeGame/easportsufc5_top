If you are stepping into the Octagon in EA SPORTS UFC 5, understanding EA SPORTS UFC 5 fighter ratings is the fastest way to pick a dominant competitor. Ratings are expressed as an overall star score out of five, and they separate good fighters from the truly elite. Based on EA's official roster hub and community ratings breakdowns, the game features a select group of 5-star fighters—including cover stars Israel Adesanya and Alexander Volkanovski—while most other top contenders sit at 4.5 stars.

## What Are EA SPORTS UFC 5 Fighter Ratings?

Every fighter on the default UFC 5 roster receives an overall rating out of five stars. A 5/5 rating represents the absolute best fighters in the game, while 4.5/5 is typically reserved for elite contenders and former champions. Lower ratings, such as 4/5 or 3.5/5, indicate solid but less complete fighters.

EA's [official UFC 5 roster hub](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ufc/ufc-5/roster-hub) invites players to check out UFC 5 fighter ratings and highlights a dedicated 5-Star Fighters category. Meanwhile, [Operation Sports' published UFC 5 ratings breakdown](https://www.operationsports.com/ea-sports-ufc-5-fighter-ratings/) revealed the top five men and women in each weight class in October 2023, noting that EA's site originally listed the top 5 per division before the full roster was revealed. Third-party roster trackers like [Dexerto's UFC 5 roster list](https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/ufc-5-roster-full-list-of-fighters-classes-more-2354974/) also list fighter specialties—such as Kickboxing, Boxing, Jiu-Jitsu, and MMA—alongside these star ratings.

## Every Confirmed 5-Star Fighter in UFC 5

The highest honor in the game is a perfect 5/5 star rating. According to EA's official 5-Star Fighters gallery, the following legends and champions are showcased with 5-star status: Georges St-Pierre (Legacy), Joanna Jedrzejczyk (Legacy), Jon Jones, Alexander Volkanovski, Amanda Nunes, and Khabib Nurmagomedov. Israel Adesanya is also confirmed as a cover star and, per Operation Sports' divisional data, holds a 5/5 rating at Middleweight.

Community-sourced data from Operation Sports expands the list of 5/5 fighters to include:

- **Fedor Emelianenko** – 5/5 (Heavyweight)
- **Islam Makhachev** – 5/5 (Lightweight)
- **Anderson Silva (Legacy)** – 5/5 (Middleweight)
- **Valentina Shevchenko** – 5/5 (Women’s Flyweight)

Not every division has a 5-star fighter. For example, the men's bantamweight and flyweight top fives are led by 4.5/5 fighters like Sean O'Malley and Alexandre Pantoja, respectively.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How are EA SPORTS UFC 5 fighter ratings calculated?

Ratings are presented as an overall star score out of five. A 5/5 rating marks the game's most elite fighters, while 4.5/5 and lower ratings indicate slightly narrower skill sets or lower overall proficiency. EA's official roster hub and community sources like Operation Sports use this five-star scale.

### Who is the highest-rated fighter in EA SPORTS UFC 5?

There is no single highest-rated fighter because multiple competitors share a perfect 5/5 star rating. Jon Jones, Fedor Emelianenko, Khabib Nurmagomedov, Islam Makhachev, Alexander Volkanovski, Israel Adesanya, Anderson Silva (Legacy), Georges St-Pierre (Legacy), Amanda Nunes, Valentina Shevchenko, and Joanna Jedrzejczyk (Legacy) all hold 5-star ratings.

### Do all weight classes have a 5-star fighter?

No. While heavyweight, light heavyweight, lightweight, featherweight, and middleweight all feature 5/5 stars, the men's bantamweight and flyweight divisions top out at 4.5/5. In women's divisions, bantamweight, flyweight, and strawweight all have a 5/5 star leader.

### Can created fighters reach a 5-star rating?

Your created fighter does not start with a fixed overall rating like the default roster. According to EA's official beginner tips, you can develop a custom fighter in Career Mode by choosing a weight class and fighter type, then leveling up attributes such as stand-up and submission through gameplay progression.
