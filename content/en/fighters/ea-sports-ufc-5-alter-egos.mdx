## Latest EA SPORTS UFC 5 Alter Egos from Content Updates

New Alter Egos arrive through periodic content updates tied to real-world UFC events. Two of the most recent and detailed updates come from UFC 322 and UFC 315 Fight Week.

### UFC 322 Update (Nov. 15, 2025)

UFC.com announced a content update on Nov. 14, 2025, with the new Alter Egos going live on Nov. 15, 2025, ahead of UFC 322. This “All Action” update celebrated several fan-favorite fighters:

- **Robbie Lawler** returned with his UFC 40 Alter Ego, highlighting the Hall of Famer’s early Octagon ferocity, including a quick victory over Tiki Ghosn that showcased the relentless striking of his career.
- **Dan Hooker** joined the roster with his UFC 305 Alter Ego, recreating his back-and-forth battle against Mateusz Gamrot that ended in a split-decision victory and Fight of the Night honors.
- **Dan Henderson** returned with his UFC 100 Alter Ego, commemorating the middleweight legend’s iconic H-Bomb knockout of rival Michael Bisping.
- **Amanda Nunes** rounded out the Alter Egos with her UFC 232 version, celebrating her legendary title fight against Cris Cyborg, where “The Lioness” became the first woman to defeat Cyborg in the Octagon via a stunning first-round knockout.

The same update also introduced rising middleweight **Roman Kopylov** as a new rostered fighter in EA SPORTS UFC 5, making his debut ahead of his UFC 322 matchup against Gregory Rodrigues.

### UFC 315 Fight Week Update

EA’s UFC 315 Fight Week update focused on the World Extreme Cagefighting era. It added four new WEC-era Alter Egos to the game: **Chael Sonnen**, **Dustin Poirier**, **Jose Aldo**, and **Cub Swanson**. Alongside these throwbacks, welterweight **Bryan Battle** was officially added to EA SPORTS UFC 5 as one of the newest rostered fighters.
