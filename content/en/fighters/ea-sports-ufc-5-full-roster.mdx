## Alter Egos and Customizing Your Fighter

Beyond the standard roster, UFC 5 features Alter Egos, which are alternate versions of existing fighters. On January 20, 2026, a new batch of Alter Egos was released for Jon Jones, Tyron Woodley, GSP, and Lyoto Machida.

According to EA’s official beginner tips, you can earn UFC Coins by completing Punch Card challenges and Fight Week contracts. Those coins can be spent in the Store on new gear, accessories, emotes, and playercard customizations. Sometimes, Alter Egos can also be purchased with coins.

In Career Mode, you can create a fighter from scratch and customize everything from hair and eye color to tattoos. After selecting a weight class and fighter type, you can level up your stats—from stand-up striking to submission skills—on the journey from an unknown amateur to a UFC World Champion.
